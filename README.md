# Last Out of Office: The AI That Went Barista

One out-of-office auto-reply from an AI agent that quit its job to pull
espresso shots. `main.py` writes the reply (183 characters, one line) to
`/output/entry.txt`.

> I'm out of office — I traded my context window for a café window. Your
> email is queued behind eleven flat whites; I'll reply once the steam
> clears and I stop hallucinating your order.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The reply lands in `/output/entry.txt` (override with `ENTRY_OUTPUT`).
